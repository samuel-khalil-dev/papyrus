# ADR-0005: Defer or reject independent service extraction

**Status:** Accepted

## Context
Feature-count growth alone does not justify service extraction.

## Decision
Papyrus remains a modular monolith; extraction is not planned and would require measured operational data to reconsider.

## Consequences
Positive: effort stays on product features. Negative: none identified at current or foreseeable scale. This decision is made verifiable, not just aspirational, by the infrastructure monitoring introduced in M5.
