# Vision & Positioning

## Statement of vision
Papyrus is a permanent, self-authored record - a digital scroll - through which its owner's professional work and technical writing are preserved and presented. Black and gold form the chromatic language of permanence and value; Pharaonic Egypt is the cultural reference, expressed through restraint rather than costume.

## Positioning
Papyrus is a purpose-built platform with an owned domain model and control plane, not a template, a third-party blogging account, or a static site. Its architecture, security posture, and operational discipline are part of what it communicates to a technically literate visitor.

## Primary audiences
1. Recruiters and hiring managers - need immediate identity clarity and credible evidence of capability.
2. Peers and technical readers - need a fast, distraction-free reading experience.
3. The owner, as sole Admin user - needs speed and clarity, not decoration.

## Language reach
English and Romanian are the initial supported locales. Arabic is an intended future locale, chosen deliberately rather than left as a generic possibility: it connects directly to the platform's Egyptian/Pharaonic identity and the owner's own linguistic background. Because of this, right-to-left layout support is built into the localization foundation from the start rather than retrofitted later (see ADR-0008).
